Description

This brownie recipe is an EASY crowd pleaser! Taking only ten minutes to whip up and twenty to bake, you can make this delicious, fudgy treat in a snap!

  • Prep Time: 10 mins
  • Cook Time: 20 mins
  • Total Time: 30 mins
  • Servings: 16
  • Yield: 16 brownies

Ingredients

  • baking spray
  • 2 cups white sugar
  • 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup walnut halves
